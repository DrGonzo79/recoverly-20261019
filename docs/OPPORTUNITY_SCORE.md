# Opportunity score

Weekly-winner weighting: pain 30%, willingness to pay 25%, evidence quality 20%, distribution fit 15%, CTA testability 10%. Each factor is scored 0–5; weighted total is out of 100.

| Factor | Score | Weight | Evidence and uncertainty |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Pain | 5/5 | 30% | Reported 35% invoice incidence and ~1% overcharge magnitude collide with 2.8%–4.0% restaurant margins. Source methodology was not independently audited here. |
| Willingness to pay | 4/5 | 25% | AP recovery firms use 10%–40% contingency and the report proposes 25%. No restaurant has accepted Recoverly’s offer. |
| Evidence quality | 4/5 | 20% | Two industry studies, operator complaints, established competitors, and public-company context triangulate the problem. Several figures are secondary citations. |
| Distribution fit | 2/5 | 15% | Zero solution-search demand and relationship-heavy CFO sales make cold distribution poor. An industry insider may score higher; this prototype has no warm channel. |
| CTA testability | 5/5 | 10% | “Send 90 days of one vendor’s invoices for a free recovery report” is concrete, measurable, and close to value. Data-sharing friction is the uncertainty. |

**Weighted total: 82/100**
Calculation: `(5/5×30) + (4/5×25) + (4/5×20) + (2/5×15) + (5/5×10) = 82`.

**Confidence:** medium. The pain is well supported; founder-specific distribution and actual acceptance remain unproven.
