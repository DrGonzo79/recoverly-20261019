# Office Hours — Recoverly

**Mode:** startup diagnostic, pre-product. **Position:** build only as a concierge recovery service with software leverage; do not start as generic restaurant AP SaaS.

1. **Demand reality:** invoice leakage is documented and recovery auditing is a paid category. There is no evidence that a restaurant CFO has authorized Recoverly to pursue a claim. The strongest next proof is a signed contingency authorization, not a waitlist.
2. **Painful status quo:** a controller exports invoices, hunts for the contract or last agreed price, calculates line-level deltas, emails the rep, follows up repeatedly, and later checks whether a credit landed. The incumbent is this human chase plus spreadsheets—not the variance dashboard.
3. **Desperate customer:** the CFO/controller of a 10–50 location restaurant group with $5M+ annual distributor spend, no dedicated recovery analyst, and margin pressure. A missed credit directly hurts unit economics; a false claim risks a strategic supplier relationship.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** a free 90-day lookback on one distributor, followed by human-approved recovery of the top five unambiguous claims for 25% of cash or credits actually received.
5. **Observation plan:** recruit ten CFOs through warm restaurant-finance introductions. Watch three controllers reconcile a live invoice batch without coaching. Record where contract evidence lives, how exceptions are judged, and how credits are verified. Success is two signed audit authorizations and at least one collected supplier credit; “interesting” does not count.
6. **Future-fit:** invoice parsing becomes commodity. Recoverly becomes more essential only if it accumulates defensible evidence lineage, supplier-specific claim playbooks, response timing, and recovery outcomes. If it remains an alert dashboard, incumbents erase it.

## Premises challenged

- **“AI agent” is not the product.** Trusted recovery is the product; deterministic math, evidence, and human approval come first.
- **Contingency pricing removes procurement friction but creates operational risk.** Revenue arrives late, disputes require labor, and recovered-value attribution must be contractually precise.
- **A broad all-vendor platform is premature.** Start with one distributor, one group, and clearly contracted SKU prices.

## Approaches considered

| Approach | Effort | Risk | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Concierge audit + evidence packet | S | Medium | **Chosen.** Fastest route to real recovery behavior and trust learning. |
| Self-serve variance dashboard | M | High | Rejected; crowded and stops before the valuable work. |
| Autonomous multi-vendor recovery agent | XL | Very high | Deferred; ambiguity and supplier relationships make autonomy unsafe pre-proof. |

**Assignment:** obtain one anonymized contract/invoice pair and permission to return a dollar-denominated recovery packet within seven days.
